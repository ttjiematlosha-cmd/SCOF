Who we are

A community forum built on access and trust.

Sasolburg Community Opportunity Forum (SCOF) is a community-focused initiative aimed at connecting people to information about employment, education, skills development and other opportunities.

We believe that access to information, communication and connections can help people make better decisions about their next steps.

SCOF is a connection point. Employers and opportunity providers remain responsible for their own selection criteria, recruitment processes and final decisions.
